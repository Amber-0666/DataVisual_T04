Data Source: 
The dataset was obtained from the Australian Government's appliance energy rating data and contains information about televisions available in Australia, including screen technology, screen size, brand, power consumption, and star ratings.

Data Processing: 
Data cleaning and preparation were performed using KNIME. This involved removing unnecessary fields, handling missing values where appropriate, and creating aggregated datasets used in the visualisations.

Privacy:
The dataset contains product information only and does not include any personal or sensitive information. Therefore, there are no privacy concerns associated with its use.

Accuracy and Limitations: 
The accuracy of the visualisations depends on the quality and completeness of the source dataset. Some television models may be outdated or no longer available in the market. Additionally, energy consumption values may differ from actual household usage due to variations in usage patterns and environmental conditions.

Ethics: 
The visualisations are intended to present information objectively and accurately. Care has been taken to avoid misleading representations of the data and to ensure that findings are supported by the underlying dataset. Users should interpret the results as informational rather than as recommendations for specific products or brands.
