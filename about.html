About This Site

This website was created as part of a university data visualisation lab exercise. Its purpose is to practise core HTML, CSS, and JavaScript skills, gain experience using GitHub for version control, and learn how to host a website. The website also serves as a platform for presenting data visualisations developed for related coursework tasks.
The website focuses on Australian appliance energy consumption, particularly television products available in Australia. Through a series of interactive visualisations, users can explore patterns and relationships in the data, such as screen technologies, screen sizes, brand distributions, power consumption, energy ratings, and other factors that influence household energy use. These visualisations help users gain insights into the Australian TV market and support data-driven analysis of energy efficiency trends.
