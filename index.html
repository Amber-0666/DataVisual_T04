Appliance Energy Consumption in Australia

Australian households rely on a growing number of electrical appliances every day, from refrigerators and washing machines to entertainment systems and heating/cooling units. Understanding how much energy these appliances consume — and what that means for household power bills and national electricity demand — is an important part of energy literacy.

This site is a placeholder demonstration built to practise front-end web development skills (HTML, CSS and JavaScript), version control with GitHub, and website hosting. It also serves as a home for data visualisations created for other coursework tasks relating to Australian appliance energy use.

Why it matters

Residential electricity use makes up a significant share of Australia's total energy demand, and appliance efficiency directly affects household running costs.

What's on this site

Placeholder content and space for charts and graphs exploring energy use trends for common household appliances, starting with televisions.
